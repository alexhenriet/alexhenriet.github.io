Side projects

A few projects built outside work, with or without AI assistance. Their repositories are private, on Bitbucket.

  • Kube File Transfer

    WinSCP-style cross-platform desktop application, in Go and Wails, for transferring files between a workstation and OpenShift / Kubernetes pods without oc.exe. Simple connection: a token copied from the web console, or the one from oc.exe if it is installed. Two-pane interface, local machine and pod side by side: drag and drop, click or F5 to transfer. Transfers through the Kubernetes exec API (tar, cat or rsync, depending on what the pod offers), with progress tracking, resume after interruption and conflict handling. Test-driven development, with 100% coverage of the business logic.
